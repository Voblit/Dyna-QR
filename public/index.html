Dynamic QR codes!

Make a redirect QR code, and edit it afterwards without having to regenerate the code. Completely free and self hosted, no propriatary nonsense like tinyurl or bitly

make a new redirect

input a short address and the full URL, and get the QR. You'll be able to change the full URL that the short address points to later, but changing the short address will unfortunately require a new QR code.

How does this works

The QR code points to a short address, that is linked to the longer address. You can then come back and reassign the short address to a new URL without any new QR codes!.
